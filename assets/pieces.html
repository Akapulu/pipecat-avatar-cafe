A video call with three people in it: you, the voice bot, and the avatar. Audio and video move over WebRTC.

Voice to text, live

Deepgram Flux writes down your words while you are still talking, so the text is ready the moment you stop.

03 · turn-taking

Are you done?

"I'd like a…" then a pause is not the end. Flux reads the words and the silence, and waits until you really finish.

Reasoning off, so it answers fast. Your prompt, your menu, your rules. Swap the model in one line.
